The Builder

About

Backlit Electric is a one-man show out of Austin Texas. I've been plugging away at music in Austin since 2001.

In the early days, the need for gear and a lack of proper funding drove me to the garage. I built gear with every extra dollar I could get my hands on. Over the years I've built a mountain of guitars, amps and pedals for local musicians, friends, family, bandmates, and randos on the internet.

I'm still stuck in the classics — the tried and tested construction methods from 50 years ago. I like thin nitro finishes, untraditional color mediums, cloth-covered wire, tru-oil, reclaimed lumber and experimental pedal schematics.

There is dark magic in taking a piece of gear from your mind to your hands.
